# Home page (app/pages/index.vue)

Scope: the home route. Mode: Persuade. Visitor: a KL/Selangor building owner or facilities manager deciding whether Setia is the contractor to call. Action: Explore a floor, read the story, or get a quote. Proof: the four registrations behind the work (CIDB G7, Suruhanjaya Tenaga Class A, ISO 9001:2015, Daikin dealership) and the 1990 founding. Constraint: the hero's Explore camera zoom measures the building against the viewport and must keep working.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The night scene stays put and the day rises over it. Scrolling reveals one mint chapter, "Committed since 1990", carrying the company's proof, then the enquiry and footer. It refuses stacking the whole About page under the hero and refuses the icon-card feature grid.

OWN-WORLD: The incumbent Setia world. Night green stage (#061710 / #0b3022), mint bands (#d1e4d7) with pine ink (#0b3022) and tinted muted text (#365b46), Hanken Grotesk body, Georgia serif for story chapters, hairline rows, underlined arrow links, pill buttons.

STORY: The visitor reads the pulse line, sees the building, scrolls. The mint band slides up over the dimming night scene. They read who Setia is and the four registrations that back it, follow "Read our story" to About, or go straight to a quote in the enquiry chapter.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The existing hero, unchanged, plus a quiet bottom-centre "Scroll" cue in the service-page grammar.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: The pinned hero. The chapter slides over it and the scene dims as it is covered (scroll-linked). Explore still measures the pinned building; if the page is scrolled, it settles to the top before the camera moves.

RISK: A hero taller than the viewport on short phones must not trap scrolling (measured negative sticky top). Browsers without scroll-timeline simply skip the dim.
