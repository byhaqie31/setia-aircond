## Marks

Marks are pale transparent (#ebf3ea) WebP variants of each client's published
identity, stored at `public/images/commercial/clients/<slug>.webp`. Clients without
a mark on disk are flagged `logo: false` in the data and show a monogram
placeholder until one is added.
